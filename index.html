My Autobiography

Yuan Miguel V. Mendoza || BSIT 3-AIML

About Me

My name is Yuan Miguel V. Mendoza, but most people call me Yuan. I am currently a college student taking up Bachelor of Science in Information Technology, majoring in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning. I study at Southville International School and Colleges here in the Philippines. I have always been drawn to technology, especially building software that solves real problems. Outside of coding, my hobbies are music production, playing musical instruments, playing video games, watching basketball, and playing sports like badminton and table tennis. I would describe myself as a simple, family-oriented person who is also curious, hardworking, and detail-oriented. One of my strengths is problem-solving under pressure. Something I am still working on improving is time management, especially when juggling multiple deadlines. I grew up in Las Piñas City, where I spent most of my childhood. My favorite subject in school has always been programming, since it lets me build functions in a system instead of just studying its theoretical foundation.

About My Family

I am an only child in my family. My father works as an OFW Seaman, while my mother is a housewife. Since I am their only child, I have a close relationship with both of my parents. We currently live here in Las Piñas City, where we have built many meaningful memories together. My parents have always been supportive of me, especially when it comes to my studies. They encourage me to do my best and remind me to stay focused on my goals. They also taught me the importance of being respectful, responsible, and hardworking. Whenever I experience difficulties in school, I can always rely on my parents for advice and encouragement. Although we can sometimes disagree with one another, we always try to understand each other and resolve our problems calmly. During our free time, we enjoy spending time together at home and talking about our experiences. We also enjoy eating meals together whenever our schedules allow us to. On weekends, we sometimes go out, visit relatives, or simply relax at home. My family has taught me to value honesty, kindness, patience, and perseverance. They have also taught me that success does not come easily and that I should continue working hard even when things become difficult. I am grateful for the sacrifices my parents make to provide for our family and support my education. Their love and encouragement motivate me to become a better person and to achieve my dreams. Overall, my family is an important part of my life because they are my source of strength, guidance, and motivation.

My Goals in Life

My goal in life is to become a successful Artificial Intelligence Engineer. I became interested in artificial intelligence because I am fascinated by how technology can learn, analyze information, and help people solve real-world problems. As a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology student, I want to develop my knowledge and skills in programming, machine learning, computer vision, and other areas of artificial intelligence. I know that becoming an AI Engineer will not be easy because it requires patience, continuous learning, and a strong understanding of technology. However, I am willing to face these challenges because I believe that every difficulty can help me improve. I want to become an engineer who does not only create advanced technology but also creates technology that is useful to society. One of the projects that motivated me to be in the AI industry is my prospective thesis project, Aqualert AI. Through this prospective thesis, I became more interested in how artificial intelligence can be used to address real problems in the Philippines. Aqualert AI combines artificial intelligence, computer vision, predictive analytics, GIS mapping, and large language models to help assess flood conditions and road passability. The project showed me that AI can be more than just a modern technology because it can potentially help people make better and safer decisions. I also have other AI/ML thesis ideas in mind, particularly around AI applications in road safety, forensics, and education, as secondary options. In the future, I want to create more intelligent systems that can help communities, businesses, and individuals. I also want to improve my programming skills so that I can turn my ideas into working applications. I plan to continuously study and practice because technology is constantly changing. I want to learn from my mistakes instead of being discouraged by them. I also hope to work with other talented people because I believe that great technology is often created through teamwork and collaboration. Eventually, I want to gain enough experience to work professionally as an AI Engineer and contribute to meaningful technological projects. I would also like to use the knowledge I gain to create solutions that can benefit people in my own country. My goal is not simply to have a high-paying career, but to have a career that gives purpose to the skills I have developed. I want my future work to show that technology and artificial intelligence can be used responsibly to improve people's lives. I know that I still have a long way to go, but I am willing to learn step by step. With determination, hard work, and continuous learning, I believe that I can achieve my dream of becoming an AI Engineer. Ultimately, I want to become someone who uses technology not only to build intelligent systems but also to make a meaningful difference in the world.
